# Quality Rubric — how this plan will be scored

A deterministic validator scores every generated plan out of 100 before the teacher sees
it. Anything that fails is sent back to you for repair. Write to pass the first time.

| # | Check | Weight | Passes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Timing integrity | 15 | stage minutes sum exactly to the lesson duration |
| 2 | Measurable objectives | 15 | every objective's main verb is on the Bloom tables and none is banned |
| 3 | ABCD completeness | 10 | every objective carries a condition and a degree |
| 4 | Bloom spread | 10 | ≥2 distinct levels, ≥1 at Apply or above, ≤2 at Remember |
| 5 | Named methodology | 5 | a recognised method is named, not described vaguely |
| 6 | Script-level procedure | 10 | each stage has teacherDoes, studentsDo and ≥2 exact questions |
| 7 | Questions carry expected answers | 5 | every question has a non-empty expected response |
| 8 | Differentiation | 10 | concrete support AND concrete extension, both specific |
| 9 | Assessment for learning | 5 | ≥2 in-lesson checkpoints naming a technique |
| 10 | No fabricated citation | 10 | no code, page or chapter number appears unless it was supplied |
| 11 | Context fidelity | 5 | local names, rupees, no-tech fallback for any powered resource |

## Common failure patterns, and the fix

- **"Students will understand fractions."** → banned verb, no condition, no degree.
  Fix: "Students will be able to compare two fractions with unlike denominators using a
  fraction wall, correctly for at least four of five pairs."
- **Stages summing to 48 in a 40-minute lesson.** → shorten Development, not the
  Evaluation. Recount.
- **"Ask questions from the students."** → not script-level. Give the exact questions.
- **"Weak students will be given extra help."** → not concrete. Name the help: "a
  half-completed fraction wall and a partner who has already mastered it".
- **"Refer to page 47."** when no page was supplied → fabricated. Say "the unit named in
  the brief".
- **"Use a projector to show a video."** with no fallback → add "or draw the three
  stages on the board as a labelled cycle if there is no power".
- **All five objectives at Remember.** → raise at least one to Apply and one to Analyse.
