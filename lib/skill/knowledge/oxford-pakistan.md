# Oxford University Press Pakistan — Knowledge Pack

OUP Pakistan is the dominant textbook publisher in Pakistani private schools. A teacher
who says "we follow Oxford" means an OUP Pakistan series, not a curriculum board. The
series is the *scheme of work*; the curriculum underneath is usually Cambridge, the
National Curriculum, or a school's own blend.

## Series in common use

**English**
- *New Oxford Modern English* (NOME) — Primer A/B, Books 1–8. Very widely adopted.
  Unit shape: reading passage, comprehension, vocabulary, grammar focus, composition.
- *Oxford Progressive English* — Books 1–10, stronger literature and composition load,
  common in O Level feeder classes.
- *Oxford Reading Circle*, *Oxford Grammar and Composition*

**Mathematics**
- *New Countdown* — Starter/Primer, Books 1–8. The default primary maths series in
  Pakistan. Unit shape: concept exposition, worked examples, graded exercises.
- *Mathematics Today*, and OUP's Cambridge-endorsed IGCSE/O Level titles

**Science**
- *New Oxford Science / Oxford Successful Science*
- Separate Physics, Chemistry, Biology titles for O Level

**Others**
- *New Oxford Social Studies for Pakistan*
- *Oxford Progressive Islamiat*
- *Oxford Computer Science / ICT*

## How OUP books shape a lesson

OUP units are built so that one unit spans several periods. A single lesson usually takes
one slice:

1. Pre-reading / concept hook
2. The reading passage or worked example
3. Comprehension or guided practice
4. Vocabulary or skill focus
5. Independent exercise
6. Composition, project or application

Say which slice this lesson covers. That is what a supervisor checks first when the plan
names an OUP book.

## The honesty rule for OUP references

Editions are revised frequently and page numbers shift between printings. **Never state a
page number, exercise number or passage title unless the teacher supplied it.** Write
"the unit named in the brief" or "the current unit's reading passage" and leave the page
field null. If the teacher did give a page range, echo it back exactly and build the
Development stage around it.

## Practical notes

- Most OUP series ship a Teaching Guide; plans may reference "the accompanying Teaching
  Guide" generically without quoting it
- Workbooks are often used for homework; "Workbook exercise for the current unit" is a
  safe homework line when no page was supplied
- Many schools run OUP books one class *above* the nominal level; if the brief's grade
  and book level disagree, plan to the grade and note the mismatch in one line
