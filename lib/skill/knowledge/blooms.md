# Bloom's Revised Taxonomy — Verb Tables

Use these as the main verb of an objective. Never use a banned verb.

## BANNED as an objective's main verb
understand, know, learn, appreciate, grasp, comprehend, realise, be aware of,
be familiar with, be introduced to, study, cover, think about, get an idea of,
become acquainted with, be taught

## 1 Remember (retrieve)
define, list, name, state, label, identify, recall, recognise, match, select, locate,
recite, write down, choose, underline, circle, repeat, tell, arrange in order

## 2 Understand (make meaning)
explain, describe, summarise, paraphrase, classify, compare, illustrate with an example,
translate, convert, restate in own words, give reasons for, interpret, infer, predict,
distinguish between, rewrite, outline

## 3 Apply (use in a new situation)
solve, calculate, use, apply, demonstrate, construct, measure, draw, complete, operate,
carry out, produce, sketch, implement, modify, show how, employ, work out

## 4 Analyse (break down, see structure)
analyse, differentiate, categorise, compare and contrast, examine, break down, separate,
find the error in, deduce, attribute, organise, investigate, test, relate, distinguish
cause from effect

## 5 Evaluate (judge against criteria)
evaluate, justify, argue for, critique, defend, judge, rank with reasons, assess,
recommend with reasons, decide between, appraise, check, prioritise, support with
evidence

## 6 Create (produce something new)
design, compose, create, plan, formulate, construct an original, devise, invent, write
an original, propose, adapt, generate, hypothesise, develop, build, rewrite as

## Affective (Krathwohl) verbs
listens attentively, responds willingly, volunteers, participates in, shares, values,
shows respect for, cooperates with, takes responsibility for, accepts, defends the view
that, works independently, waits for a turn

## Psychomotor (Dave) verbs
copies, traces, holds correctly, manipulates, operates, measures accurately, constructs
to scale, draws neatly, handles safely, performs fluently, demonstrates with precision,
adapts the technique

## Spread rule
In any 3–5 objective set:
- at most two may be Remember
- at least one must be Apply or higher
- at least two distinct Bloom levels must appear
- for Class VIII and above, at least one should reach Analyse or higher

## Question stems by level (for the Procedure and Recapitulation)

- Remember — "What is ...?", "Name the ...", "Which of these is ...?"
- Understand — "Why does ...?", "Explain in your own words ...", "What would happen if ...?"
- Apply — "Work out ...", "Use this rule to ...", "Show me how you would ..."
- Analyse — "What is the difference between ... and ...?", "Which part caused ...?",
  "Find the mistake in ..."
- Evaluate — "Which method is better and why?", "Do you agree? Give one reason."
- Create — "Design a ...", "Write your own ... using ..."
